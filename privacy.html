Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Cornelius is a personal assistant operated by and for a single individual (the account owner). It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, Cornelius accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events through Google APIs to read, organize, and draft email and to read and manage calendar events.

How data is used

  • Data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Email is never sent without the owner's explicit approval.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. No Google user data is stored on any server operated for this app.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/imaboveyou/cornelius-app.
